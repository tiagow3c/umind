## src/lib rules
- Processing pipeline (src/lib/processing.server.ts) runs one stage per call (transcribe → diarize → align → analyze → embed), driven by the meeting page polling AND the managed cron schedule hitting /api/public/process-queue every minute (bounded batch of 3, single-flight lease + circuit breaker in public.job_state; 402/403 pause the processor, user retry resumes it). Runs are idempotent per (recording, stage, input_version, config_version) with lease + optimistic claim. Analyze's and embed's input_version is the current canonical transcript id so corrections trigger a fresh analysis and re-embedding.
- Long recordings are split into ~24 MB chunks (src/lib/chunking-rules.ts) over confirmed recording_parts; completed text is checkpointed on processing_runs.output.chunkTexts and resumed without replaying completed chunks.
- Semantic search uses google/gemini-embedding-2 (3072 dims, stored as halfvec) on transcript_segments and analysis_items; queries go through the search_segments/search_items SQL functions which re-check workspace membership.
- Canonical text/timing/speakers come from pyannoteAI /diarize (precision-3, exclusive turns, transcription enabled); words follow exclusive turns and clean ≤15 s samples are stored on speakers. /identify uses precision-2 voiceprints and meaningful temporal overlap. Job ids/results persist for polling; configured Deepgram fallback is allowed only without pyannote or on confirmed terminal diarization job failure, never credit/access/unknown errors.
- People registry: public.people is the workspace-wide directory; participants.person_id links a meeting participant to it (person_confirmed=false means AI suggestion). The analyze stage links AI-heard names via matchPerson (src/lib/people-rules.ts, never guesses on ambiguity) or creates the person; cross-meeting voice matching: naming a speaker enrolls a pyannote voiceprint (clip cut in the browser, public.person_voiceprints); diarize uses /identify with the workspace voiceprints and align links matches (confidence >= VOICE_MATCH_MIN) as mapping_source='voice', unconfirmed.
- Speaker identification cascade lives in the align stage (right after transcription): voiceprints → inferSpeakersFromConversation (src/lib/people-rules.ts, nearby vocative+reply and self-intro votes, tested) → a small AI naming call; voice matches remain suggestions until confirmed, and only confirmed identities enroll a voiceprint through resumable voice_enrollments. Identity failure must not block a valid transcript.
- Day-column to-dos (Tarefas page, /tarefas; meeting suggestions live only inside each meeting page) live in public.todos, owned per user; rollover of unfinished items to today is computed on read in src/lib/todo-rules.ts (tested), never by rewriting rows.
- Voiceprint clips are peak-normalised in the browser and near-silent clips are rejected, because raw capture (no auto gain) produced degenerate voiceprints that never matched.
- Tasks are written to the owner (people.is_self) in first person: work owned by others is stored as action.assignee="follow_up" (tested in src/lib/task-rules.ts) so it is never shown as the owner's task; action.kind="forward" carries a Gmail search_query and is executed by src/lib/gmail.functions.ts through the owner's connected Gmail.
